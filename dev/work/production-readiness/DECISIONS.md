# Production Readiness Decision Log

Append-only record of the dated decisions and reviewed findings behind the production-readiness work. Each entry states the decision or finding, its limits, and links to evidence. Do not rewrite or delete entries; add a new dated entry to supersede an earlier one. Plans and gate documents link here instead of restating these facts.

This log is not legal advice or a compliance determination.

## 2026-10-18

- **Operating model recorded:** ScriptSyncPilot is maintained and published from Norway by an individual for worldwide use as a free, MIT-licensed, source-only distribution with no commercial activity and no service provided. Keep releases source-only unless this model is reassessed.
- **License and contribution terms adopted:** MIT, `Copyright (c) 2026 Frank Yong`, confirmed with the maintainer. Contributions are accepted under MIT, contributors retain copyright, and no CLA is required. See [LICENSE](../../../LICENSE) and [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../../CONTRIBUTING.md).
- **Third-party attribution inventory completed:** the application projects use in-repository project references with no runtime NuGet packages or bundled third-party assets; test-only package licenses/notices are recorded in the [third-party components inventory](../../../docs/third-party-components.md). Recheck when dependencies, assets, or the distribution model change.
- **Preliminary CRA position:** based on the European Commission's [CRA summary](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/cra-summary) and [open-source guidance](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/cra-open-source), the current free, non-monetised, source-only FOSS distribution appears not to be made available on the market in the course of commercial activity and therefore appears outside the CRA; the individual maintainer does not meet the stated legal-person condition for an open-source software steward. This is a reasoned preliminary position, not a formal determination. Reassess if monetisation, commercial distribution/support, a steward arrangement, or other relevant facts change.
- **HandySync data flow documented and verified against source:** Handy REST requests include the connection key and application identifier in headers; the app uploads a CSV containing only funscript action timestamps/positions to Handy's Hosting API v2, receives a temporary download URL, and sends that URL plus playback/synchronization timing to HSSP. Video and app settings remain local. See [HandyApiClient](../../../src/ScriptSyncPilot.HandySync/HandyApiClient.cs) and [Funscript.ToCsv](../../../src/ScriptSyncPilot.Common/Funscript.cs). The [Hosting API spec](https://www.handyfeeling.com/api/hosting/v2/docs/spec.yaml) describes uploads as temporary but gives no exact expiry/deletion period.
- **Telemetry inspection:** no telemetry or crash-reporting code/SDKs were found in the application source or explicit package references. The maintainer reports not receiving or controlling personal data. These findings do not determine what Handy independently collects or what users might submit through external support channels.
- **Accountability assigned:** the GitHub repository owner is accountable for issue and security-report triage, publishing releases, any older-version support policy, and communicating breaking changes.

## 2026-10-24

- **Initial support scope approved** by the maintainer, including limited automatic recovery for the first release. See the [support scope and known limitations](SCOPE-AND-LIMITATIONS.md). Firmware updating is out of scope across all ScriptSyncPilot applications: apps may report compatibility requirements and documentation may link to official manufacturer instructions, but users perform updates through official vendor channels.
- **Tested environments recorded:** [ENV-001](TESTED-ENVIRONMENTS.md#env-001) records two H01 devices (model number 0, variant 0), firmware `4.3.0+dcee7e58` and `4.2.2+44874492`, with successful workflows confirmed by the maintainer; connection/setup corroborated by logs. This is limited coverage, not broad device certification; compatibility claims must not exceed it.
- **Initial-release reliability priorities approved** by the maintainer: (1) timeouts and shutdown, (2) error reporting without false success or secret disclosure, (3) command ordering across disconnect/session replacement, (4) firmware/capability gating. Automatic reconnect, device-takeover/session restoration, expanded retry/backoff, and a diagnostics overhaul are deferred and are not initial-release requirements. Verification order only; not evidence that verification is complete.
- **Initial-release scope classifications recorded** in the [product vision](../../VISION.md#initial-release-classification), [shared backlog](../../BACKLOG.md#initial-release-scope-classification), [HandySync backlog](../../HandySync/BACKLOG.md#initial-release-scope-classification), and [editor backlog](../../FunscriptEditor/BACKLOG.md#initial-release-scope-classification). Existing feature priorities remain future-work priorities, not release commitments.
- **Paid legal review declined** by the maintainer due to cost. This is a budget decision, not a finding that no obligations apply; GDPR and Norwegian/EEA applicability remains unresolved, and disclaimers do not replace applicable duties. Reassess if the operating model changes.
