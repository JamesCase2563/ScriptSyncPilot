I’d start with the **pattern insertion tool on the existing single timeline**. It has immediate value and doesn’t require the larger multitrack/project-format redesign.

A useful first version:
- Enter counted cycles and observation duration; calculate cycles per minute.
- Choose triangle, fast-up/slow-down, or slowed direction changes.
- Set movement range and insertion duration.
- Preview before applying.
- Prompt **Stop / Replace / Merge** only on conflicts.

Before implementing, define what Merge does at identical timestamps and add focused tests for timing and conflict handling. Ideally, make insertion undoable as one operation.

After that, tackle **chapter display and navigation**, followed by the **multitrack foundation**. Audio-to-funscript conversion should come later: it needs that foundation and more decisions about how audio maps to movement.
