Both plans aim at the same decision, but they're different kinds of documents. Here's my read:

## Overall verdict

The proposal (`PLAN-proposal.md`) is a clear improvement **as a forward-looking release document**. The current `PLAN.md` has done its job — most of its checkboxes are done, and what remains is verification and a go/no-go. The proposal correctly recognizes that and restructures around **release gates with minimum evidence**, which is exactly the right shape for the remaining work. I'd adopt it, with a few fixes below.

## What the proposal does well

- **Gates instead of checklists.** The gate table with "minimum evidence" + "release blocker?" columns is far more decision-oriented than milestone 3's mixed list of done/undone tasks. It also designates `VERIFICATION.md` as the single evidence location, which PLAN.md only mentioned in passing.
- **Faithfully preserves the hard-won caveats.** I checked each against PLAN.md: the preliminary (non-formal) CRA position, "budget decision, not a finding that no obligations apply" on the declined legal review, "communication loss prevents guaranteeing a physical stop," "no new command-coalescing machinery required," "limited coverage, not broad device certification," and "no claim of compliance without adequate basis" all survive intact. That's the most important thing a rewrite could have lost, and it didn't.
- **Better-bounded open items.** "Make *one documented attempt* to ask Handy about retention… if they don't clarify, record it as unresolved and don't promise a retention period" is a tighter, more completable formulation than PLAN.md's open-ended ask. (Related: `HANDY-DATA-RETENTION-EMAIL.md` already exists — the proposal should link it as the attempt's artifact.)
- **~60% shorter** with all links resolving (I verified every referenced file exists).

## What gets lost — worth fixing before adoption

1. **The dated decision trail.** PLAN.md records *when and by whom* decisions were made (scope approved 2026-10-24, legal review declined 2026-10-24, credential work archived 2026-10-23). The proposal drops the dates. Since this plan partly exists to show diligence, either keep the dates in "Decisions and evidence already recorded" or **archive PLAN.md rather than delete it** (the repo already has an `dev/archive/` convention — move it there when the proposal is adopted).

2. **The GDPR assessment is quietly demoted.** PLAN.md has an explicit open task: *"Assess GDPR and Norwegian/EEA data-protection obligations… determine whether privacy information, lawful-basis/role analysis, contact point, or other process is required."* The proposal converts this into an open *risk* ("has not been determined… reassess if the operating model changes"). That's a real scope change — from "do an assessment" to "don't claim anything unsupported." It may be the right call given the budget decision, but it should be an explicit, acknowledged downgrade, not an artifact of summarization. I'd add one sentence to the Privacy gate or the risk noting this is a deliberate narrowing.

3. **CI gate is vaguer.** PLAN.md required coverage for "lifecycle, protocol handling, persistence, and failure paths"; the proposal says only "runs the required tests." Without naming the coverage areas, "required tests" is whatever happens to exist. Carry the four areas into the gate text.

4. **Two small drops:** PLAN.md's "withdraw **or** supersede a faulty release" became just "supersede" (keep both — withdrawal of a tagged source release is a distinct action), and the Backlog Overlap table's nuance about session/UI separation ("only extract boundaries where reliability warrants it") disappeared entirely — minor, but it was a guard against scope creep.

## One structural observation

The proposal's "Reliability acceptance details" section largely duplicates the reliability gate row and PLAN.md's priority table. That's fine, but pick one canonical form — I'd keep the priority-ordered table from PLAN.md (priorities 1–4 + deferred) since verification will be executed in that order, and let the gate row link to it.

**Bottom line:** adopt the proposal, add the decision dates (or archive PLAN.md alongside it), name the CI coverage areas, restore "withdraw," and make the GDPR demotion explicit. Want me to apply those edits to the proposal?
